## 01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE CODE

I am **Benard Ochieng**, a software engineer and technology entrepreneur interested in building useful, scalable digital products.

My journey into technology began with a background in Molecular Biology, followed by Information Technology training and full-stack software engineering. This combination shaped how I approach problems: analytical thinking, structured research and practical engineering.

I work across the software development lifecycle, from identifying a problem and designing its architecture to implementation, deployment and ongoing maintenance.
